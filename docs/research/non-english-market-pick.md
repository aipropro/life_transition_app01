# Non-English market pick: where to launch after the English markets

**Date:** 5 September 2026
**Product:** Gray-Divorce Money + Admin Reset (90-day one-time purchase, women 50+)
**Question:** Which single non-English-language market is the biggest and best next launch, and why?

## Verdict

**#1 Japan.** Largest pool of long-marriage divorces among high-spending app markets, the highest iOS share and per-capita app spend of any candidate, a hard legal deadline that most divorced women currently miss (only about 18% claim the pension split), a fresh April 2026 law change to launch on, a proven one-time-purchase category, and a moderate, predictable privacy regime.

**Runner-up: Germany.** Largest EU pool, high spend, the strongest women-finance channel found anywhere (Madame Moneypenny, herMoney), and the GDPR/DSA build is reusable for Spain, Italy and France. Loses to Japan because the German pension split is run by the court automatically, which removes the sharpest deadline.

**Third: South Korea.** The biggest whitespace (no divorce-prep or checklist app exists at all) and the most painful procedure (a claimant-driven pension split with a rule that can wipe the claim to zero). Held back by a 75% Android base, the heaviest privacy-consent regime of any candidate, and a shrinking total divorce count.

## Japan: what the app must cover

Working name for the category in Japanese: 熟年離婚後の手続き (procedures after a mature-age divorce). Related search terms: 離婚後 手続き チェックリスト, 年金分割, 財産分与, 離婚準備アプリ, 手続き管理.

Stage 1: before and at the divorce filing (離婚届)
- 年金分割のための情報通知書: request from the pension office (年金事務所) before or right after the divorce to see the split range.
- Choose the route: 合意分割 (agreement up to 50%, by notarised deed or family-court mediation) or 3号分割 (automatic 50/50 for periods as a dependent spouse after April 2008, unilateral filing).
- 公正証書 for the property division and any pension-split agreement.
- Decide the surname: revert to the maiden name by default, or file 離婚の際に称していた氏を称する届 within 3 months to keep the married name.

Stage 2: within 14 days of the divorce or move (municipal office, 市区町村)
- 国民年金 category change from 第3号 to 第1号 (or join an employer plan).
- 国民健康保険 enrolment after leaving the spouse's health insurance as a dependent, or join own employer plan.
- 住民票 and 世帯変更届, 転出届 / 転入届.
- マイナンバーカード name and address update.

Stage 3: pension and money deadlines
- File 標準報酬改定請求書 for the pension split within 2 years of divorce (divorces before 1 April 2026) or 5 years (on or after). The split covers the earnings-related 厚生年金 portion only.
- Property division claim (財産分与) period: 2 years, extended to 5 years under the 2024 Civil Code amendment effective 1 April 2026 (confirm with counsel).
- 慰謝料 claims: 3 years.
- Housing loan and title: 名義変更 (registration), remove guarantor status, bank negotiation.
- Life-insurance beneficiary change, will (遺言書) update, bank and card name/address.
- Tax: 寡婦控除 eligibility check, 配偶者控除 ends, year-end adjustment or 確定申告; national pension contribution exemption application if income is low.
- Later: old-age pension claim timing; note 振替加算 stops after divorce.

Stage 4: 90-day reset
- Cash-flow snapshot with the new pension projection.
- Export a one-page packet for an FP, 社労士, or 弁護士.

Local price test: ¥4,980 vs ¥9,800 one-time (about USD 33-65). The movell ¥1,500 anchor makes the US price of $99-149 unrealistic in Japan; FP simulations at ¥33,000 leave room above ¥1,500.

Channels to test first: FP firms with divorce desks, certified divorce counsellors (認定離婚カウンセラー), YouTube creators covering 熟年離婚 and 年金分割, the 2026 deadline-extension news cycle, and 年金事務所 or municipal counters (leaflet or QR partnership).

Main risks
- Price anchor at ¥1,500 and free law-firm checklists.
- Legal content must be reviewed by a 社労士 or 弁護士; three 2026 law changes (共同親権, 財産分与 period, 年金分割 period) mean ongoing maintenance.
- My Number and tax-slip masking; APPI foreign-operator duties from the 2026 reform.
- Japanese support and UX expectations; older users favour large type and simple flows.
- The addressable pool is stable, not growing: about 40k long-marriage divorces a year.
