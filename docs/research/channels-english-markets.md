## First ten to approach

1. Divorce After Fifty (Facebook): pinned first-30-days checklist, no selling.
2. 50+ Women Moving Forward After Divorce (Facebook): co-hosted paperwork-triage session.
3. Divorced Women Over 50: Thrive (Facebook): in-group mini-course using the app.
4. She's Not Done Yet (Substack): sponsored series plus subscriber trial.
5. Women's Financial Wellness Center (CDFA): branded onboarding for her clients.
6. divorcemoneyprep (TikTok): 30-day clip series, each clip tied to one workflow.
7. Women Living Well After 50 (Australia): episode and after-decree pack for the Australian module.
8. Silver Divorce Podcast: first-30-days logistics episode.
9. r/AskWomenOver50: high-quality answers, links only where rules allow.
10. Worthy's Facebook-group list: approach admins with group-only access, not ads.
