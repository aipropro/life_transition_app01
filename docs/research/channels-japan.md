## Facts confirmed in this run

- Pension-split claim period is 5 years for divorces on or after 1 April 2026, 2 years before that (日本年金機構).
- Property-division claim period is 5 years for divorces on or after 1 April 2026 under Civil Code Art. 768 (家庭裁判所).
- 3号分割: a dependent spouse can split the employee-pension record for periods after April 2008 unilaterally, 50/50.
